## Standard Armory

# (d100)

**Roll 1d100 when a player searches an armory locker, crate, or wall rack.**

* Common weapons/ammo (1–40)
* Military gear (41–70)
* Rare / Exotic SCP-tech (71–90)
* Very Rare / Prototype SCP items (91–100)

---

### **1–40: Ammunition & Basics**

* **01–05:** Box of 12 Gauge Slug Shells (2d6 shells)
* **06–10:** Box of 5.56 NATO (2d20 rounds)
* **11–15:** Box of 7.62x39mm (2d20 rounds)
* **16–20:** Belt of .50 BMG ammo (2d10 rounds)
* **21–25:** Gauss Rifle Ammunition (1d4 rounds) *(if no Gauss Rifle, keep in storage)*
* **26–30:** RPG-7 HEAT Missile (1d2 rockets)
* **31–35:** Standard Frag Grenade (1d2)
* **36–40:** Tear Gas Grenade (1d3)

---

### **41–70: Standard-Issue Weapons & Gear**

* **41–45:** Combat Knife (1)
* **46–50:** Remington 870 Fieldmaster (comes with 1d6 shells)
* **51–55:** M16A4 Rifle (comes with 1 loaded mag, 20 rounds)
* **56–60:** AK-47 WASR-10 (comes with 1 loaded mag, 20 rounds)
* **61–65:** M2A1 Browning (tripod included, 1 loaded belt, 50 rounds)
* **66–70:** Standard Kevlar Armor (Helmet + Vest set)

---

### **71–90: Advanced or SCP-Integrated Weapons**

* **71–74:** SCP-127 "Organic MP5K-PDW" (starts with full mag of 60 organic rounds)
* **75–78:** Ghost Thrower (1d6 charges)
* **79–82:** RPG-7 Launcher (if not already found, includes 1 HEAT rocket)
* **83–86:** Teleportation Gauntlet (requires Atomic Battery to function)
* **87–90:** Exosuit Attachment (roll 1d4 for module type)
  1. Auto Defense Turret Plugin
  2. Advanced Sensor Suite Plugin
  3. Combat Shield Generator PLugin
  4. Thaumic Spellcasting Plugin

---

### **91–100: Prototype / Very Rare SCP Items**

* **91–93:** Gauss Rifle (with 1d4 rounds of gauss ammo)
* **94–96:** High-Frequency Blade 
* **97–98:** SCP-6952-3 Hephaestus-Grade Armor (Odd Plugin, Even Personnel)
* **99–100:** Experimental Cache: GM’s Choice (roll twice on this table, combine results into one "strange loadout" — e.g., an M16 with SCP-tech attachment)



# 🔒 High Value Item Storage Loot Table (d20)

**Roll 1d20 when searching High Value Item Storage.**

---

### **1–10: Common Anomalies**

* **01–05:** SCP-170 Superglue (permanent, unbreakable adhesion).
* **06–10:** SCP-447 Green Slime (harmless unless near corpses → catastrophic reaction).

---

### **11–15: Uncommon Containment Goods**

* **11–12:** Sealed Vial of SCP-409 Crystal Dust (causes crystallization if spilled).
* **13–14:** Miniaturized Containment Cube (holds 1 Small object/creature in stasis for 24h).
* **15:** Prototype Stun Gauntlets (+1d6 lightning on melee hit, 1d6 charges).

---

### **16–18: Rare Technology / Prototypes**

* **16:** Cloaking Harness (short-range active camouflage, 3 charges/day).
* **17:** Thaumaturgic Lens (see hidden anomalous runes/sigils).
* **18:** Reality Anchor Core Fragment (+2 saves vs. mind/illusion for 1d6 hrs, but unstable).

---

### **19: Very Rare**

* **Flipper Zero (Hardened Foundation Edition)**

  * Can spoof keycards, crack electronic locks, and brute-force passcodes.
  * 3 uses/day before recharge at a Foundation terminal.

---

### **20: Ultra Rare**

* **Butterfly Control Module**

  * SCP-grade neural interface.
  * Grants command over a swarm of anomalous butterflies (GM-defined).
  * Dangerous to user and highly sought-after.
